4. Food Safety & Kitchen Cooking Disclaimer / 食品安全與烹飪免責條款

⚠️ Critical Culinary Safety Notice / 重要廚房安全與衛生須知：
Malaysian cuisine relies on authentic cooking techniques including intense open-flame wok hei (鑊氣), deep-frying in hot oils, heavy stone mortar pounding, and pressure steaming. By using this application, you acknowledge and agree to the following safety precautions:
  • High Heat & Oil Splatters (高溫烈火與滾油燙傷防護): Dishes like Char Kway Teow, Pisang Goreng, and Curry Puffs require extreme oil temperatures (180°C–200°C). Always maintain adequate distance, use protective splatter screens or aprons, never pour water into hot oil, and ensure dry hands/ingredients before placing food into oil.
  • Handling Fresh Chilies & Pungent Spices (辣椒素與辛香料防護): Malaysian Sambal, Cili Padi (bird’s eye chili), and dried shrimp paste (Belacan) contain high concentrations of capsaicin and pungent volatile oils. When crushing or blending in a mortar (lesung batu), wear kitchen gloves, avoid touching your eyes or face, and wash hands thoroughly with soap immediately afterwards.
  • Allergen Warnings & Cross-Contamination (過敏原重度警示): Authentic Malaysian food widely uses common major allergens, including shrimp paste (Belacan), dried anchovies (Ikan Bilis), peanuts (Satay sauce), tree nuts (Candlenut/Buah Keras), shellfish/blood cockles, soy, and coconut milk. Users with allergies must carefully inspect packaging and substitute ingredients appropriately.
  • Safe Internal Cooking Temperatures (生熟食交叉感染與安全核心溫度): Poultry (Hainanese Chicken, Satay skewers), pork (Klang Bak Kut Teh), and seafood must be cooked to international USDA/FDA recommended minimum safe internal temperatures (Poultry: 165°F / 74°C; Pork: 145°F / 63°C). Use separate cutting boards for raw meats and ready-to-eat garnishes.
  • Pressure Cooker & Claypot Safety (壓力鍋與砂鍋防爆防燙): When using Instant Pot or electric pressure cookers for Rendang or bone soups, never force open the lid until steam has completely depressurized naturally or via designated safety release valves. Traditional claypots must not be exposed to sudden cold thermal shock to prevent cracking.
  • Instructional Nature: All recipes, timing calculators, and temperature guides are provided for general home cooking education and culinary appreciation only. Users assume full personal responsibility for their food preparation, food safety, kitchen equipment operation, and dietary choices.
